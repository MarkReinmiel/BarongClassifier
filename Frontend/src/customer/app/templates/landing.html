About the Barong Tagalog

The Philippines’ national formal garment

The Barong Tagalog is a formal shirt traditionally worn untucked, often over an undershirt, and finished with fine embroidery.

It is commonly woven from natural fibers such as piña and jusi, and remains a symbol of Filipino heritage and occasion wear.

The fabrics

Fabrics we can identify

Piña

A fine, lustrous fabric made from pineapple leaf fiber.

Jusi

A sheer, crisp fabric with a smooth, flat finish.

Cocoon

A textured fabric with a natural, uneven look.
