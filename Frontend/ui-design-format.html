Copy this into a prompt

Design a screen for Barong Classifier, a web app that identifies Barong Tagalog textiles (Piña, Jusi, Cocoon) from a photo.

Visual system:
- Background #E5DED4, cards #FFFFFF, soft fill #F5F1EC
- Text #2F323A, secondary text #5D5B63
- Accent plum #77567A, deep plum #49344D, highlight mauve #C47AC0
- Borders #D5CBD2 / #B9A9B9, soft shadow
- Headlines: Playfair Display. UI and body: DM Sans
- Eyebrows are small, uppercase, letterspaced, in the plum accent
- Buttons are pills. Primary is plum with white text. Highlight actions use mauve
- Brand mark is a diamond ◆. Tagline: “Preserving Heritage Through Technology”
- Footer words: CULTURE | HERITAGE | INNOVATION
- Mood: formal Philippine heritage, warm stone and plum, generous whitespace, rounded 16–20px cards
- App nav: Dashboard, Classify Textile, Textile Types, History, About, plus an account menu
- Do not use bright blue, neon, or generic SaaS purple gradients

Mobile (under 800px, phone width about 390px):
- One column. Center public pages. Hide the tagline under the logo
- Public Home: hamburger plus a small Get Started in the bar. Hero text, full-width Get Started, then the photo, then CULTURE | HERITAGE | INNOVATION
- Sign in: brand block centered on top, form card full width underneath, Back to Home above the brand
- Signed-in bar is short. Links hide behind a hamburger. The open menu is a full-screen dark panel (#292b32) with large rounded links. Active link uses a mauve tint
- Dashboard, Textile Types, History, and About stack their cards vertically
- History filters are one field per row. Today, Filter, and Clear sit in one row of three
- Classify: Camera and Upload from Gallery stack. Analyze Textile sticks to the bottom of the screen
- Camera frame is portrait 3:4 with a colored guide (red, amber, or green). Capture Image is full width
- Result stacks the photo above the detail rows. View Image is full width
- Touch targets at least 48px. Respect the phone safe area
